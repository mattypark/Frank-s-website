*Written for Axiom Pathways.*

Obama's 2008 campaign tested 24 combinations of homepage image and button text across 310,000 visitors and found a black-and-white family photo paired with the softer "Learn More" button beat the stock page by 40.6% — an estimated $60M in extra donations from two changes. The piece uses that experiment to argue a website isn't a brochure but a conversion machine, and that every visitor is silently asking four questions in the first few seconds (what is this, is it for me, can I trust it, what do I do next) — laying out page speed, a specific headline, one clear low-commitment call to action, and visible trust signals as the four levers that actually move conversion.
