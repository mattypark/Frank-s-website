*Written for Axiom Pathways.*

A single viral tweet sent Arcads.ai from $5,000 to $64,000 in monthly recurring revenue. The company turns a script and a library of 1,000+ AI actors into a UGC-style video ad in about two minutes — riding the same authenticity that makes user-generated content outperform polished studio ads on TikTok and Instagram. This debrief digs into why Eurazeo led its $16M seed, what's genuinely disruptive about its fully autonomous ad-scraping and generation loop, and the real blind spot: Arcads doesn't own its underlying generation model, leaving it dependent on third-party infrastructure it doesn't control.
