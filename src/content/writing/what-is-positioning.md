*Written for Axiom Pathways.*

Drawing on Peter Thiel's argument in *Zero to One* that great startups avoid competition by specializing until alternatives become irrelevant, this piece traces how Facebook's Harvard-only launch, Amazon's decision to start with books, and Netflix's early DVD-by-mail model were all instances of the same strategy: win a narrow, well-understood group completely before expanding. The lesson frames positioning as work that should happen before the product itself — find the pain point, own the niche, and price accordingly.
