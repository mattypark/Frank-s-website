*Written for Axiom Pathways.*

In February 2026, Alexander Liteplo built a marketplace in a day and a half to solve a specific gap: AI agents could handle digital tasks but had no way to act in the physical world. RentAHuman connects AI agents to human workers via Anthropic's Model Context Protocol, and hit 40,000 sign-ups in three days off a provocative reframe — what happens when AI becomes an employer? This debrief covers why YC funded a company that inverts the usual AI-replaces-jobs narrative, what makes its MCP-native architecture genuinely different from Fiverr or TaskRabbit, and the blind spot underneath it: a trust system that doesn't reliably guarantee workers deliver, and a long-term bet that the same AI progress fueling its growth could eventually automate away the need for it.
