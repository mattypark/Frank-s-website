*Written for Axiom Pathways.*

Emily Weiss spent four years blogging real skincare interviews on Into The Gloss with zero budget before Glossier existed as a product — by launch, her audience already knew the brand's point of view so well that 80% of early customers heard about it from a friend, no ads required. The piece uses her story to redefine "brand" as not a logo or color palette but "the conversation people are having about you when you leave the room" — built through consistent point of view repeated across every free interaction, long before there's money to spend on it.
